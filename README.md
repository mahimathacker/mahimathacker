## 🙋🏻‍♀️ About Me

I am a Full-Stack Engineer and Developer Educator with 4+ years of experience building Web3 applications, AI-agent tooling, SDKs, CLIs, automation workflows, and developer-facing products. I started coding at 15 and have grown through engineering, content, and community-focused roles.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **Web3, AI tooling, developer education, and product engineering**. My work includes DApps, smart contracts, MCP servers, x402 payment tooling, n8n automation, Solana tooling, technical content, and workshops for developers.

I care about making complex technologies easier to build with. Whether I am shipping a tool, writing a technical breakdown, mentoring hackathon teams, or running a workshop, my goal is to help more developers learn, experiment, and build useful things.

---

## 🚀 What I Build & Share

- 🧠 **AI agent tooling** - MCP servers, AI-agent workflows, tool-calling experiments, and automation systems
- 🛠️ **Developer tools** - SDKs, CLIs, npm packages, n8n nodes, docs, examples, and onboarding resources
- ⛓️ **Web3 applications** - DApps, smart contracts, DeFi flows, x402 payments, and Solana/EVM tooling
- ✍️ **Technical content** - blogs, threads, video explainers, diagrams, and beginner-friendly protocol breakdowns
- 🎓 **Developer education** - workshops, study sessions, community learning, and mentoring for students and builders
- 🤝 **Community work** - hackathon mentoring, ecosystem contribution, and grassroots Web3 education
